Role descriptions

Owner

Full access to all features. Can manage team members, settings and billing.

Admin

Can manage customers, invoices, payments, products and view reports. Cannot manage team or billing.

Staff

Can view customers, create invoices, record payments and view reports. Limited access to settings.
